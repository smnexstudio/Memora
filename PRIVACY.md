# Privacy

Memora has no servers, no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Everything you put in it stays on your phone unless you send or save it yourself.

## What Memora stores, on your phone only

| What | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Names, dates, event types, years | To remind you | App's private database |
| Phone numbers and email addresses (optional) | To open WhatsApp, SMS or email with your wish | App's private database |
| Your messages, notes, gift ideas, cards sent | So you can see what you sent and plan the next one | App's private database |
| Settings, app-lock PIN (as a salted hash only) | To work the way you set it | App's private database |
| Festival dates | To remind you of festivals you switched on | App's private database |
| Card pictures | To hand them to WhatsApp or email | App's cache, deleted after a day |
| A short diagnostics log | To work out why a reminder didn't arrive | App's private files; actions only, never names, dates or numbers |

Notes are free text. Think before writing health details or anything else sensitive in them, especially if you save backups without a password.

## Network use

Memora connects to the internet for exactly two things, and sends nothing about the people in it:

- **Festival dates**, only after you switch festivals on: Google Calendar's public holiday feeds, over HTTPS. Google sees your IP address and which holiday calendars you picked, which can include a religion's holidays if you chose one. No Google account is used.
- **Update checks**: GitHub's public releases API, once a week (turn off in Settings → Updates), and the download itself if you choose to update. GitHub sees your IP address and Memora's version.

## Permissions

- **Contacts**: read only when you import or look up a number. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Notifications, alarms, full-screen alarm**: to remind you on time.
- **Install apps**: only to install a Memora update you confirm.

## What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

- Wishes and cards you send go to WhatsApp, your SMS app or your email app, and their privacy terms apply from there.
- Backups go wherever you save them. Protect them with a password: an unprotected backup can be read by anyone who has the file, including a cloud folder's provider.
- Cards you save to your gallery (the "save a copy" option when sending) are visible to other apps that can see your photos. Settings → Cards → **Delete cards saved to the gallery** removes them.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Backup and restore → **Erase everything** removes all events, notes, history, settings and the PIN from Memora. Uninstalling Memora removes everything too. Backups and cards you saved elsewhere stay where you put them.

## People in your Memora

Memora holds details about other people (your friends and family). It's meant for your own personal use; if you use it for a business, data-protection law may give those people rights you'd be responsible for.
